## Research Implementation

During the research and implementation phase, a prototype Hybrid AI Customer Support System was developed to practically analyze how AI can improve customer service efficiency and customer satisfaction.

The implementation focused on NLP-based query processing, sentiment analysis, intent classification, confidence scoring, and human escalation workflow. Different customer scenarios were tested to observe how the system responds to normal and emotionally sensitive queries.

The prototype demonstrated that AI performs efficiently for repetitive customer queries, while frustrated or dissatisfied users require human interaction for better resolution and emotional understanding.

Therefore, a hybrid approach was implemented where the system automatically escalates low-confidence or negative-sentiment conversations to human support agents.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Hybrid AI systems can improve customer service operations by combining AI automation with human assistance. The model provides fast automated responses for common queries while ensuring that emotionally sensitive or unresolved issues are escalated to human agents for a more personalized customer experience.
